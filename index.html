// About Me

Bridging Intelligence and Data

1+ Years Research
1 Publications

I'm a PhD student at TU Wien in the field of artificial intelligence, and a Research Scientist at Unlimidata. I am part of the Bilateral-AI research project, focusing on neurosymbolic artificial intelligence.

My research interests are particularly focused on the study of Large Language Models & Agentic AI in the context of Data Curation and Quality. I aim to reshape data reliability by researching and building AI-driven tools for automated data curation and quality assurance.

Before joining TU Wien, I completed my studies at the University of Calabria in computer engineering specializing in AI & Machine Learning.

// Research Areas

Core Research Pillars

My work spans two interconnected domains, unified by the goal of creating intelligent systems that can reliably operate in industrial environments.

Agentic AI & LLMs

Designing autonomous agents capable of reasoning, planning, and executing multi-step tasks. Focus on tool use, memory architectures, and human-in-the-loop workflows for industrial process automation.

  • Multi-agent orchestration
  • Tool use & function calling
  • Planning & reasoning
  • Human-AI collaboration
Agents LLMs Automation

AI × Database Integration

Bridging intelligent agents with structured data infrastructure. Developing systems for data quality rule discovery, automated data checking, semantic schema understanding, and intelligent data pipelines.

  • Data Quality Rule Discovery
  • Automated Data Checking
  • Semantic data discovery
  • Knowledge-grounded querying
Databases Data Quality

These two pillars converge in my thesis: enabling autonomous AI agents to reliably interact with enterprise database systems using large language models.

Trustworthy & Fair Agentic RAG

A Multi-Aspect Agent-Based RAG system designed to integrate Trustworthiness and Fairness properties via an orchestration of specialized LLM agents.

NLP RAG Agentic AI Trustworthy AI

ABCU Benchmark

ABCU (Aspect-Based Column Understanding) is a benchmark created for the homonymous paper introducing this novel task. ABCU focuses on the semantic interpretation of table attributes, aiming to uncover what each column represents in the real world and how its values are expressed, aggregated, or related to others, according to specified semantic aspects.
